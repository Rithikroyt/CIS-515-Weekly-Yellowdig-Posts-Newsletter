What to carry forward

A high score isn't proof

A judge passed a reply that broke policy. Hard rules belong in code or with a person.

Cap the loop, then hand off

Give every retry loop a maximum and a place to go when it runs out.

Test the failure path on purpose

Raise the threshold or feed a known-bad input to see what your safeguards catch.
